Ping Pong Player

Project Overview

This project involves a HiWonder arm interacting with a dynamic environment using computer vision, trajectory planning, and inverse kinematics to enable an opponent in engage in pingpong gameplay.

MVP & Stretch Goals

Our MVP is to have a fixed robot arm block a ping pong ball rolling towards it. The arm should be able to track and predict the ball’s trajectory, plan out the kinematics for where to go to contact the ball, and successfully make it there in time. This will work across varying ball speeds. This lends itself to varying improvements: introducing a “swing” to return the ball instead of just blocking it, or potentially catching the ball as an advancement in complexity without completely changing the function. Our stretch goal will involve a fixed robot arm hitting a ping pong in mid air with a given type of swing that aims the ball towards one direction (left, middle, or right side) of the table. Each swing should be successfully completed at a rate of over 50% accuracy.
